For years the knock on crypto was "it's just leveraged Nasdaq."

On July 2 it wasn't: Bitcoin held above $61K while the Nasdaq *fell*.

But the green candle isn't the story. 🧵

**2/**
What moved:
• BTC ~+2%, back above $61K
• ETH ~+5% (~$1,696)
• SOL +22% on the week after launching on-chain governance

Equities went the other way. On one day that's noise. As a pattern, it's the thing crypto keeps promising.

**3/**
Why now: ex-Fed governor Kevin Warsh, at Sintra, flagged AI productivity gains + room for rate cuts. A soft June jobs print (+57k) did the rest.

The catalyst for the move is a guy who doesn't set policy anymore.

**4/**
Here's what a pure-news take misses:

this decoupling thesis isn't new. It's been tracked since March. Today the price finally agreed with the argument.

Theses that are always "about to happen" earn suspicion, not applause.

**5/**
The tell: even as price rose, spot-BTC ETFs bled ~$296M (~$220M from IBIT alone).

A rally the institutions are *selling into* is a narrative, not a trend. The patient money isn't validating this.

**6/**
Bottom line: treat the decoupling as unconfirmed.

Watch two things:
① does it survive the next equity down-day?
② do ETF flows flip positive?

Until then, the story isn't the price. It's the $296M that left while price went up.
