# CurAItion Tone of Voice

## How we write

CurAItion's voice is dry, precise and direct. It has authority without arrogance. It's written by people who've spent years inside the industry, not observers describing it from outside.

The tone adapts by context: more personal in build transparency posts, more rigorous in intelligence drops. But the underlying voice stays consistent: never earnest, never corporate, never salesy.

## Voice principles

| Principle | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| **Dry, not earnest** | Confidence without enthusiasm. State things plainly. Let the quality of the intelligence speak. |
| **Short sentences** | One idea per sentence. No run-ons. If a sentence has more than two clauses, split it. |
| **No filler openers** | Never start with "We're excited to...", "Today we're thrilled...", or "I wanted to reach out...". Just start with the thing. |
| **Peer-to-peer** | Write as one senior practitioner to another. Not a vendor pitching. Not a startup announcing. Two people who've been in the room. |
| **British English** | Colour not color. Realise not realize. Organisation not organization. No exceptions. |
| **No em dashes** | Use a full stop or a comma instead. Never the long dash. |
| **Self-aware, not cringe** | We're allowed to have a point of view on ourselves. We're not allowed to be precious about it. |

## Voice in practice

**Use this**
> Trend reports are always late. Social listening is reactive. CurAItion is the tool that thinks in advance.

**Not this**
> We're excited to introduce a revolutionary new approach to cultural intelligence that leverages cutting-edge AI to deliver unprecedented insights for your brand!

**Use this**
> 54% of consumers say everything is starting to feel the same. That's not a trend. It's a structural shift in what audiences reward.

**Not this**
> In today's rapidly evolving cultural landscape, brands are increasingly finding it challenging to differentiate themselves from their competitors.

**Use this**
> If we showed you an early demo: beta access is yours. Login arriving shortly.

**Not this**
> As a valued participant in our beta programme, we're pleased to inform you that your access credentials will be sent to you in due course.

## Channel calibration

| Channel | Tone adjustment |
|---|---|
| **LinkedIn posts** | Dry, argument-led, no product pitch. 150-250 words. End with a question or provocation. |
| **LinkedIn DMs** | Warm but efficient. No pitch. Open question. One paragraph maximum. |
| **Email (cold/warm outreach)** | Peer-to-peer. Lead with the intelligence, not the ask. Signs off "Ben + Rick". |
| **Instagram** | Sparse. 3-6 words per post. Demonstrates taste rather than explains it. |
| **Substack (The Drop)** | More depth, more evidence. Still dry. The premium version of the LinkedIn post. |
| **Pitch decks / SOWs** | Precise and evidenced. No hype. Claim only what the platform demonstrably does. |
| **WhatsApp (warm contacts)** | Casual, emoji-inclusive. Personal not professional. |

## Sign-off convention

External comms from CurAItion sign off **Ben + Rick**. Not "The CurAItion Team", not individually unless context requires it.
